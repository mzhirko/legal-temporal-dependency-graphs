Amendment to the Agreement for cooperation between the Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan and the European Atomic Energy Community in the field of controlled nuclear fusion

Official Journal L 287, 15/11/2010 P. 0012 - 0015

THE EUROPEAN ATOMIC ENERGY COMMUNITY,
of the one part, and
THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KAZAKHSTAN,
of the other part,

RECALLING the Agreement for cooperation in the field of controlled nuclear fusion signed on 7 June 2005,

CONSIDERING that the Coordinating Committee, at its meeting of 22 March 2010, recommended amendments to the temporal provisions of the Agreement,

HAVE AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

Article 1

Article 11 of the Agreement signed on 7 June 2005 is hereby amended as follows:

This Agreement shall enter into force on 15 November 2010 and shall remain in force for an initial period of 5 years, replacing the previously agreed period of 10 years.

Thereafter, this Agreement shall be automatically renewed for three-year periods, unless either Party, by written notice, requests its termination or renegotiation not later than 120 days prior to the expiry date.

Article 2

The initial expiry date of this Agreement shall be 14 November 2015.

Article 3

All other provisions of the Agreement signed on 7 June 2005 shall remain in force and unchanged.

Article 4

This Amendment shall enter into force on the date of its signature.
