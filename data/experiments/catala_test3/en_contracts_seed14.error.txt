# Agreement Compliance Deadline

This scope calculates the deadline for submitting goods and relevant movement certificates to the importing state's customs authorities.

```catala
declaration scope MovementCertificateCompliance:
  input OriginalCertificateDate date
  output SubmissionDeadline date
```

The goods and relevant movement certificate must be produced to the customs authorities of the importing state within six months from the date of issue of the original movement certificate.

```catala
scope MovementCertificateCompliance:
  date round down
  definition SubmissionDeadline equals OriginalCertificateDate + 6 month
```
